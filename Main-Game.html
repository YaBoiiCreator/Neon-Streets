NEON STREETS

An open-world crime sandbox with a 5-mission story and a customs garage. Steal cars, cause chaos, outrun the cops. Everything here is built from code, no ripped assets.

On foot: WASD move · Shift run · Q/E or drag = camera · Space shoot · F enter car
Driving: W/S gas and brake · A/D steer · Space handbrake · F exit
Other: M map (or tap minimap) · 1-4 weapons · R reload · Z/X/V buy guns at the red beam
Phone: P or 📱 → My Vehicles (call any vehicle you own, anytime) · Jobs (optional zombie missions) · Browser → Command Truck. Progress autosaves. 🪦 Purple beam: press E to start Graveyard Survival. Press \ for online mode. Vehicle guns X · homing missile V · jammer L (all installed at the truck)
